Series Overview

What This Series Covers

This is the forensic core of our Bangladesh coverage. Since August 2024, Bangladesh has undergone a systematic transformation — institutional capture, press freedom erosion, the rehabilitation of political Islam, an interim government operating under a genuine constitutional crisis, and an economy under real strain.

We treat this as an ongoing post-mortem: each piece examines one angle — banking, fiscal policy, self-dealing, minority persecution, electoral integrity, security — rather than trying to summarize everything at once. The evidence base grows as the record grows.

This series will keep expanding. It is deliberately the deepest, most frequently updated part of this program.

Key Questions This Series Addresses

  • How have democratic institutions been systematically weakened since August 2024?
  • What is the interim government's actual constitutional standing?
  • How has political Islam, particularly Jamaat-e-Islami, been rehabilitated?
  • What happened to press freedom and civil society space?
  • What does the banking collapse and fiscal crisis actually look like in detail?
  • Who bears documented responsibility for self-dealing and elite corruption?
  • What international accountability mechanisms apply, and are they being used?
