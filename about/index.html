Our Mission

Why We Exist

To produce independent, evidence-based research on sovereignty, self-sufficiency, and the paths nations take toward shared prosperity — starting in South Asia, and extending to every region walking a similar road — and to put that analysis in the hands of the people who can act on it.

Fuence is the public face of the Futura Genesis Center — an independent research initiative studying the conditions that let nations build real sovereignty, self-sufficiency, and lasting prosperity, beginning with South Asia's democratic future and extending to every region we study. We produce reports, strategic briefs, and op-eds grounded in documented evidence. Our work is designed for the people who shape decisions: policymakers, international institutions, diaspora networks, and journalists who need more than commentary.

Our commitment is to accuracy, documented evidence, and a fair accounting of what's actually happening on the ground.

Our Vision

The World We Work Toward

A South Asia — and a world — where sovereignty and self-sufficiency are lived realities, not just declarations. Where Bangladesh's founding democratic principles endure, where nations across the region and beyond build shared prosperity through cooperation rather than dependency, and where the path forward is chosen together rather than imposed.

We work toward a region — and a world — where civil society operates freely, where communities live without fear, and where partnerships between nations are built on mutual respect and shared progress, not convenience or dependency.

Every report we publish is a step toward that shared future. The archive we build today becomes the record that tomorrow's advocates, institutions, and policymakers can build on.
